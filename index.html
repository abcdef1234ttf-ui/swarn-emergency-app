📚 Emergency Guide & First Aid Instructions
☣️ Poisoning / Snake Bite (Zeher ya Saanp Katna) Call 1066
  1. Don't Panic: Saanp katne par mareez ko bilkul hilne-dhulne na dein taaki zeher shareer me na faile.
  2. No Sucking/Cutting: Dakh-wale jagah par kat na lagayein na hi munh se zeher choosne ki koshish karein.
  3. Call AIIMS Poison Line: Immediately 1066 par call karke antidotal advice lein aur sarkari hospital jayein.
❤️ Heart Attack / CPR Guide Call 108
  1. Position: Mareez ko peeth ke bal sapat zameen par litayein.
  2. CPR Push: Dono hathon ko seene ke beech me rakh kar 100-120 chest compressions per minute ki speed se zor se dabayein.
  3. Call Ambulance: Turant 108 ko call karein.
💳 Cyber Fraud / Bank Money Stolen Call 1930
  1. Golden Hour: Fraud hone ke 1 ghante ke andar 1930 par call karein.
  2. Freeze Bank Account: Bank customer care ko call karke apna ATM/Netbanking block karein.
  3. Report Online: cybercrime.gov.in par complaint darj karein.
🔥 Fire Safety & Burns Call 101
  1. Stop, Drop & Roll: Agar kapdon me aag lage toh bhagein nahi, zameen par roll karein.
  2. Burns: Jalne wali jagah par 10-15 minute tak saaf paani dalein (Baraf na lagayein).
